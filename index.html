1. Eligibility and Registration

Players must be 18 years or older to participate. Each player requires a unique Free Fire MAX UID. One player cannot be on multiple teams. Registration is confirmed upon form submission and must be completed before the tournament deadline.

  • Roster size: Up to 4 players per squad
  • Substitutes allowed with prior notification
  • False or incorrect details lead to immediate disqualification

2. Entry Fee and Refunds

Entry fee: ₹160 per team (₹40 per player). Refunds policy:

  • Organizers cancel the tournament → Full refund
  • Team voluntarily withdraws before start → Full refund
  • Team does not show up without prior withdrawal → No refund
  • Disqualified due to rule violations → No refund

3. Match Format and Schedule

Tournament format: Squad mode on Free Fire MAX. The tournament will consist of 3 matches. Format and grouping will be decided based on the total number of participating teams. All 3 matches will be counted for prize distribution.

  • Check-in starts 15 minutes before match start
  • If a team is absent at match start for any reason, the match will NOT be restarted
  • Approximately 5 minutes break between matches
  • Match schedule may be updated with notice

4. Device and Gameplay Rules

  • Mobile devices and tablets allowed (no emulators)
  • Must use own registered account
  • No hacks, mods, third-party tools, or macros
  • No teaming with other squads or intentional feeding
  • Internet disconnections do not guarantee replays

Squad Composition Rule: The 4 players who participate in the first match must remain the same throughout the entire tournament. Substitutions are not allowed after the first match.

5. Fair Play and Disqualification

Violations of gameplay rules result in warnings, point deduction, or disqualification. Organizers' decisions are final. Disputes must be raised within 15 minutes of match end with video evidence.

6. Prizes and Prize Distribution

Revenue breakdown from entry fees:

  • 20% goes to platform (organizers)
  • 80% goes to prize pool:
    • ~70% distributed as weekly prizes to top-performing teams
    • ~10% distributed to top-performing teams on the leaderboard (cumulative)

Winners must claim prizes within 7 days. Applicable taxes are the responsibility of winners. Prizes are non-transferable.

7. Data Collection and Privacy

We collect only: player name, phone number, and team player names. This information is used for tournament administration only. Data is stored securely and not shared with third parties without consent. To request data deletion, contact any admin via WhatsApp.

8. Liability and Force Majeure

Organizers are not liable for technical failures, server outages, or device issues. The tournament can be postponed or cancelled due to circumstances beyond our control.

Server Issues Protocol: If the organizers' server experiences issues during a match and the match is exited, all team captains must post their match results in the captains WhatsApp group within 5 minutes of match completion. Failure to post results within 5 minutes will result in zero points for that round.

9. Conduct

All participants must maintain respectful behavior. Abuse, hate speech, or harassment results in removal and potential ban from future tournaments.
